Welcome

Welcome to Lega

Lega is a private space for noticing what is going on within you without needing to find the perfect emotion, label, or explanation first.

Record as much or as little as you want, revisit moments from your day, and gradually build a picture of your experiences over time.

Before you continue

A quick note about Lega

Lega is designed for personal reflection and self-understanding.

  • Lega does not diagnose mental health conditions.
  • Entries, patterns, colours, icons, sliders, and statistics should not be interpreted as medical or psychological assessments.
  • Lega is not therapy and is not a replacement for professional healthcare or mental health support.
  • The meaning of your experiences remains yours. Lega helps you reflect; it does not decide what you are feeling for you.

About

What is Lega?

Lega gives you a flexible way to record moments, sensations, and experiences without requiring you to name an emotion first.

Your timeline is a reflection of what you choose to record. It is not a diagnosis or mental health assessment.
